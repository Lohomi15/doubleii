How it works

Three steps.
No copy-paste.

1
Highlight the line you don't get

Select any passage in any article — a jargon term, a dense sentence, an unexplained acronym. Works on any website.

2
Click Explain, or use the shortcut

A small Explain button appears near your selection. Click it — or use the keyboard shortcut — and a shimmer tells you doubleii is thinking.

3
Read the explanation in context

doubleii reads the full article around your selection and explains only the highlighted line in plain language — no generic dictionary definitions.

The shortcut
macOS
⌥ Option + B

Highlight text, then press Option+B to explain instantly.

Windows / Linux
Alt + B

Highlight text, then press Alt+B to explain instantly.

Built to stay out of your way.

Context-aware, not generic

doubleii reads the surrounding article so the explanation is specific to what you're actually reading — not a Wikipedia definition lifted out of context.

READABILITY EXTRACTION

Bring your own key

Use your Anthropic or OpenAI key. Your key stays in your browser — it's never transmitted to a doubleii server (there isn't one).

Any language

Get explanations in English, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Chinese, or French. Set it once in Settings.

Every explanation is saved on your device. Come back later, see what you asked, and click "Open article" to jump straight back to the exact line.

Your data belongs to you.

doubleii was designed with a simple rule: if it doesn't need to see your data, it doesn't. And since there's no server, there's nothing to leak.

1

doubleii has no servers. There is no backend. We never receive your text, your key, or your history.

2

Your API key stays on your device — stored in chrome.storage.local, only ever sent in the Authorization header of the call you make.

3

Your history stays on your device too. Local only, never synced.

4

The highlighted text and article context go directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, using your key. Paid API tiers generally don't train on your data.

Install in 30 seconds.

Desktop only — Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Arc. No app store. Download directly from GitHub.

1 Download the latest release from GitHub and unzip it anywhere.
2 Open chrome://extensions, toggle on Developer mode (top-right), click Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
3 Pin doubleii from the puzzle-piece menu, then click it → Settings → paste your API key.
4 Open any article, highlight a line, press ⌥B (Mac) or Alt+B (Windows). Done.
No auto-updates. doubleii doesn't update itself. When a new version is released, re-download the ZIP from GitHub and repeat steps 1–2. Watch releases on GitHub to know when one ships.
