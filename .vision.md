# doubleii — product thesis

## what it is at its core

A browser extension that lives at the reading layer. You highlight something you don't understand, and doubleii explains it — in context, using the surrounding page, with no servers and no data leaving your machine (unless you're using a cloud key, in which case only the LLM provider sees it).

The product is deliberately passive. You don't open a chat window or switch apps. You just read, and when something stops you, doubleii is already there.

---

## the bet: education, not PA

There are two routes this could go.

**Route 1 — Personal Assistant.** Calendar, email, CRM, lead re-engagement, meeting rescheduling. Think Donna from Suits — aware of all your conversations and messages, taking actions on your behalf. Compelling vision, but it requires permissions, trust, and deep integrations. Crowded space (Superhuman, Notion AI, Copilot, Gmail AI). The "no data leaves with Ollama" angle is real but niche to start.

**Route 2 — Education.** doubleii infers what you're trying to learn from watching you learn. Not from what you declare — from what you highlight, what you ask about, what pages you return to. It builds a map of your knowledge gaps from behavior, then surfaces learning guides, structured topics, and chapter-level resources.

The bet is Route 2. Here's why:

1. **No one else sits at the reading layer.** Most L&D tools make you declare intent upfront. doubleii doesn't ask — it watches. That's only possible if you live inside the browser, on every page, passively.

2. **The behavioral inference angle is genuinely novel.** "You asked about LLM inference seven times across five different pages — here's a structured guide." No other product can produce that because no other product has that signal.

3. **It maps to a real underserved user.** Not students studying for exams. People who are building things, making decisions, talking to experts — and teaching themselves as they go. Non-CS founders reading research papers. Lawyers learning about a new regulatory area. Investors trying to understand a technical market.

4. **The memory system (issue #14) is already the foundation.** Once doubleii knows what you've been asking about, the learning guide is just a synthesis step away.

---

## how the PA route fits in later

The PA features aren't killed — they're deferred and repositioned. Once doubleii has strong user context (knows you're in a fundraising round, knows you've been reading about SAFEs vs. equity, knows you talk to a lawyer named X), the PA actions become contextual augmentations of that knowledge, not a separate product.

"Reply to this message" is a PA feature. But "reply to this message, knowing you've been discussing Series A terms for three weeks and the other party is your lead investor" — that's the education product doing PA work downstream.

---

## enabling infrastructure

- **Ollama support** — the privacy unlock. For anyone who won't touch a cloud key (sensitive chats, legal, medical), local models make doubleii usable. This isn't a niche — it's the feature that makes the product trustworthy to the users who would get the most out of it.
- **User memory** — passive inference + explicit "Add context." The layer that makes explanations personal instead of generic.
- **PageIndex / vector retrieval** — future: when memory grows large, retrieval replaces linear search. Same architecture as any RAG system but scoped to a single user's history.

---

## what success looks like early

Not "everyone uses it." A specific user: someone who is smart, building or deciding something important, and teaching themselves a new domain to do it. They use doubleii daily, not occasionally. After two weeks, doubleii knows more about what they're learning than any single person in their life does.

That's the wedge. Everything else follows from that.
